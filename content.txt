N4MES Brand Name N4MES Tagline MAKE IT MEAN SOMETHING. What N4MES Is N4MES is a brand-building and creative company built around ideas. It brings together brand thinking, creativity, storytelling and execution — finding the thought, story, perspective or cultural connection that can make something matter, and then bringing that idea to life. N4MES is not intended to be positioned simply as a “marketing agency.” Marketing, content, advertising, production, digital, SEO, media and other capabilities are part of what N4MES can do, but they are not the identity of the brand. The brand should feel bigger than the services it provides. What N4MES Believes Make it mean something. The belief is simple: brands can become much bigger than what they sell when there is an idea behind them that gives people a reason to care, notice, talk and remember. The right idea can change how people see the same thing. A product does not always need to change. A logo does not always need to change. The packaging does not always need to change. Sometimes, what needs to change is the meaning around it. N4MES looks for that meaning. How N4MES Thinks N4MES does not begin with: “What should we make?” 1It begins with: “What does this mean?” What is interesting here? What is the story nobody is telling? What is hidden in the product, history, culture or people behind it? What connection has nobody made yet? What could this become? The idea comes first. The medium comes later. Brand Language These are ideas and phrases that can belong to the N4MES world: • • • • • • • • • • • • Make it mean something. Find the idea. Give it meaning. Make it matter. Make it bigger. Make people look again. Give people something to remember. There's always more to it. Look closer. Find what's already there. The idea changes everything. Same thing. Different meaning. These are not necessarily taglines. They are part of the language and philosophy of the brand. Core Thought The right idea can make anything bigger. This thought should influence the way N4MES talks about its work, its clients, its ideas and its own brand. Brand Direction N4MES should feel like a brand first and a marketing company second. The website should not read like an agency brochure or a list of services. It should feel like a point of view — a company that sees brands, culture and ideas differently. The services explain what N4MES can execute. The ideas explain why N4MES exists.
